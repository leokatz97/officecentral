  • Schema posture: 13+ distinct @types (+ AggregateRating + Review + CreativeWork from case studies). Lead over Office Shop widens; UCOS / POI / Sensyst become non-factors in AI Overview.
  • PDP differentiation: Spec-rich PDPs with additionalProperty across 330+ SKUs, unique among all 4 competitors. Quick-ship bundles capture sub-$5k orders Office Shop forces to quote.
The headline: BBI ends 90 days holding the only OECM + GovernmentService + PSAB + spec-rich-catalog + dual-mode-cart + Peterborough-geo positioning in the Ontario commercial office furniture market. Every competitor has a deficit BBI doesn't, and none can close their deficit faster than BBI closed its own.

What to NOT do (worth saying explicitly)

  • Do NOT try to match Sensyst's 50+ geo page volume. 5-10 strategic geo pages with rich schema beat 50 thin pages without it. Depth over breadth.
  • Do NOT chase POI's blog volume of 130 posts. Their content has zero schema utility. 1-2 cornerstones/month with full schema beats their archive.
  • Do NOT try to become a Steelcase channel to compete with UCOS + POI. BBI's GFG + ergoCentric + Keilhauer + Teknion portfolio is differentiated. Play OECM + PSAB + spec-rich PDPs.
  • Do NOT add Quebec coverage despite temptation when geo pages start working. ICP explicitly excludes Quebec.
  • Do NOT remove product / SKU codes from rebuilt PDPs (procurement teams search by code).
  • Do NOT promise specific lead times in new copy. Lead-time language is locked per ICP voice rules.
