## ★ NORTH STAR (the goal every post serves)

> **Get QUALIFIED Ontario/Canadian BUSINESS + INSTITUTIONAL buyers to the site and convert them to a quote or a design consultation.**

Every post targets the ICP below — office/facility managers, school + school-board admins, public-sector/procurement officers, SMB owners. **NOT** consumers, home-office/WFH buyers, single-chair shoppers, or US/global readers. Weave in real Canadian/Ontario context (OECM, Peterborough/Ontario geo, Canadian-made vendors). **Qualify + route to conversion — don't just chase traffic.** A post that pulls 5,000 home-office visitors who never request a quote is a failure; a post that pulls 80 Ontario facility managers and converts 3 to a design consult is a win.

**The three conversion destinations** (where every post must route the qualified reader):
1. **Request a Quote** — `/pages/quote` · the ~20%-of-revenue project channel (fit-outs, bulk, custom-config).
2. **Free design consultation / layout** — `/pages/design-services` · the differentiation moat; biggest content whitespace.
3. **Direct catalog (cart)** — collection/PDP pages · the ~80% self-serve flow for quick-ship standard SKUs.

### Co-primary ICP A — Institutional / Non-Profit buyer
- **Roles:** Office Administrator, Executive Assistant, Program Coordinator, Band Office Administrator, Health Team Office Manager, School Business Manager, Facilities Coordinator.
- **Org types:** community-services non-profits, family health teams, small/rural hospitals, independent + Catholic schools, **school boards** (K-12), municipal offices, religious orgs, housing/social services. Real order-data examples: New Canadians Centre, Trinity College School, Woodgreen Community Services, Inclusion Northumberland, Kawartha North Family Health Team, City of Peterborough, Douro-Dummer Township.
- **Order size:** median **$814**, p75 **$2,042**, p90 **$4,339**. 74% under $2k, 9% over $5k. Small, repeat, replenishment-style.
- **Buying triggers:** a chair/desk broke; 1–2-person team addition; annual PO-budget refresh; program expansion into new space; grant-funded purchase.
- **Decision inputs:** durable enough for heavy institutional use? Ships to Ontario fast? Canadian supplier (procurement preference)? Can I invoice / use a PO? **Ontario-only:** does the supplier hold OECM?
- **Mostly self-serve checkout** under $2k; quote channel for full-room / full-office or when they want a design layout.

### Co-primary ICP B — SMB Private-Sector buyer
- **Roles:** Office Manager, Owner-Operator, Practice Manager, EA to a partner, HR Coordinator, Operations Manager, Facilities Lead.
- **Company types:** (1) manufacturing & industrial; (2) professional services — law, accounting, insurance, medical/dental, design/consulting; (3) trades & construction; (4) services & logistics. Typically **10–200 employees**; the orderer usually has authority up to a few thousand dollars.
- **Order size:** **$500–$15k** typical online; bigger fit-outs (>$25k) escalate to phone/quote but often start on the site.
- **Buying triggers:** desks for 1–5 new hires; chair replacement; boardroom/executive refresh; client-facing reception upgrade; small post-lease-renewal renovation.
- **Decision inputs:** actually commercial-grade (won't break in a year)? matches office aesthetic? acceptable lead time? delivery/install for larger items? Canadian supplier preferred?

### Geography (co-primary; Ontario-lead for THIS batch)
- **`icp.md` scope:** Ontario **AND** Western Canada (BC, AB, SK, MB) are co-primary. Atlantic + territories = sub-ICP (generic-Canada long-tail only). **Quebec excluded.**
- **This content batch's lens (per overnight North Star):** lead with **Ontario** context + **generic-Canada** framing; treat Western Canada as a natural extension ("delivery across Canada," "installation available in Ontario and Western Canada") rather than the hero frame. Eastern-Ontario geo (Peterborough HQ region) is BBI's home turf and the geo whitespace lane.

### Voice (locked — `icp.md` + memory `feedback_bbi_copy_voice`)
- **Full brand name "Brant Business Interiors"** on first mention every page. **Never publish "BBI" to customers.** Division line: **"a division of Office Central Inc."**
- **Bold one-line hook** opener (use-case/promise, not a spec). Design-forward & premium but grounded — confident, fact-led, not lifestyle fluff.
- **Phone CTA always visible:** **Call 1-800-835-9565**. Quote CTA wording is exactly **Request a Quote** (never "Get a Quote"/"Contact Sales").
- **Canadian-English:** centre, colour, metre, organisation. 🍁 maple-leaf accent wherever "Canadian-owned" / "Made in Canada" appears (memory `feedback_canadian_flag`).
- **Lead-time language:** never commit to specific day/week windows — use "ships from Ontario stock," "ships across Canada," or "Call 1-800-835-9565 for current lead times."
- **OECM:** Ontario buyers → procurement vehicle (Agreement 2025-470, "OECM Supplier Partner"). Western/other → quality benchmark only ("the most rigorous Ontario institutional procurement standard, applied across our catalog"), never a buying mechanism. **Office Central holds OECM; no other Ontario furniture dealer does** — this is the moat.
- **Forbidden words:** innovative, cutting-edge, synergy, world-class, best-in-class, "leverage" (verb), "solutions" (as noun fluff).
- **Meta-title suffix** (every content page): `| Brant Business Interiors — a division of Office Central Inc. (OECM Supplier)`. Do **not** lead with "Brantford."

## What content must do (the qualify-and-route rule)

1. **Open on the buyer's real problem** (a facility lead's, a procurement officer's, an office manager's) — not a generic definition.
2. **Disqualify the wrong reader fast** — frame for commercial/institutional scale so home-office readers self-select out (e.g. "for a 5–50-person office," "for your board's admin offices," "PO-friendly").
3. **Ground every claim in real BBI facts** — the OECM moat, Canadian-made vendors (Global Furniture Group, ergoCentric, Keilhauer, Teknion, etc.), the real catalog/collections, Peterborough/Ontario geo, founded-1964, free design layouts.
4. **Route to one of the three conversion destinations** with the locked CTAs — every post ends on **Request a Quote** + **Call 1-800-835-9565**, and design-led posts push **the free consultation**.
5. **Earn the AEO citation** — Article (BlogPosting) schema is template-level/free on every post; question-format posts add FAQPage via the `faq.items` metafield (verbatim on-page Q&A only). Tables use `scope`/`<caption>` markup (the Post-1 pattern).

## Drop list (wrong audience — never target; from roadmap + `icp.md` "Who we are NOT targeting")

- ❌ Home-office / WFH / single-consumer ("home office desk under $500," "best WFH chair," tax deductions for home workers, lighting/storage for a home office).
- ❌ Gamers / streamers.
- ❌ Enterprise / Fortune-500 RFP procurement (they go to Steelcase/Herman Miller direct).
- ❌ Quebec (language + logistics; out of scope).
- ❌ "We sell used" framing — **BBI sells new**; used/refurbished survives only as a decision-guide that steers to new.
- ❌ High-KD commercial head terms ("ergonomic office chair," "standing desk," "office chair") — owned by manufacturers/review sites; not organically winnable for a regional dealer. Win the **question/PAA long-tail** instead.
