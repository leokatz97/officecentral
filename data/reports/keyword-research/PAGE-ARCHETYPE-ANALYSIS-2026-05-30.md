=== READ-OUT FOR BBI ===
  - Collection pages drive ~35% and product pages ~1% of competitor organic traffic — category architecture + PDP SEO is table stakes.
  - Geo pages: ~2%. Where present they convert local intent — BBI's Eastern-Ontario geo pages are a direct, winnable lever.
  - Blog/content: ~0%; service pages: ~0%. Both are THIN across competitors — design/space-planning service pages + institutional content are open lanes for BBI.
  - Cross-reference with SEED-EXPANSION net-new keywords: build geo_page + service_page + oecm_page first; they pair high BBI-fit with low competitor coverage.
